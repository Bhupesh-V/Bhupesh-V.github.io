title: Ethics of dealing with a kitten
description: What would be my verdict in front of the cat god? Guilty?

I haven't been lucky with the cat distribution system yet, but a couple of days back something happened, that let me a bit troubled for 2-3 days. The door to our terrace sort of connects to our living room, around dinner time we started hearing constant kitten cries, I ignored for a couple of minutes believing they are coming from the street just outside, however that wasn't the case until I saw dad chasing down the cat to my room.

Now I have a crying kitten under my bed, not coming out, crying albeit a bit slowly. What's the safest way I can bring her out? I install MeowTalk just to see if its worth any salt, I see the message _I'm not feeling good_, my heart sinks two stories down. Then I commit yet another horrendous mistake, I open the first video that comes after googling "sounds to attract cats" on loop, it worked, god I wish it hadn't. The kitten's cries become louder for a few seconds just before she eventually poped out in front of us, as I am pausing the video to look at the kitten, in her eyes, I see confusion, disappointment and fear, just now I had committed an immoral act, I can feel it in my bone somehow, I had just fooled a sentient creature pretending someone I'm not saying god knows what in her native tongue.

Of course, I live in a family where pets aren't welcomed we have to say goodbye to the kitten, we keep a check on the street after leaving her outside, hoping an adult cat spots her. What would an adult cat even do? The cries stop after an hour or so. I would go to bed that night knowing what I had done.

After a couple of days, during my regular dopamine session on the addiction app, I spot a video featuring a collection of people saying _"Meh eh"_ to their cats and the cats respond by coming towards the orator in a hurried and curious manner.

That's it! I should have done that, but why did I feel this was a better way then just playing the same sound in a video? Personal connection maybe, yes of course, that's one possible explanation, the effort is coming from me and unlike that video I have a soul, but is that really it? I hope the kitten is okay, I have taken the liberty to forgive myself, I hope she would understand. I wonder how the distribution system would perceive this, am I guilty?
