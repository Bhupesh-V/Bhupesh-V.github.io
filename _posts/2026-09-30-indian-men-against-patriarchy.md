Women today are as free as you in terms of access to knowledge, access and

What I love is that all these slogans have different meaning based on their
context, for instance my favorite was _Hum aazad hain sirf panno par_ not
because its catchy but because how true it is. For all the men, who complain
about women aligned laws in India's constitution, especially the divorce law
bias, need to understand that society creates laws, not the other way around,
what this means is implementation of laws is laughable until the society
collectively agrees about a certain topic. Arguing for a fairer treatment is
mute when the mindset of the uneducated half of the society is secretly
corrupted.

Another fav was _Beti ki location nahi, bete ka intent check karo_ even here the
dialectics of interpretation can be understood. For instance, I share my
location with dad as well when traveling b/w states or when I am out late with friends, but not
because I am scared that I will get raped, but because I am scared that I will
get robbed or murdered being the sole breadwinner.

These are 2 completely different scenarios, for women its about body autonomy on
top of the high-level evil of living in a troubled society. It's pretty easy to
recover from getting robbed (I can get 2 jobs in worse case scenario) not at all
easy to recover from getting raped.

The hilarious reality is how the most popular religion in India, has a literal
fictional war being fought over a women being harassed. However, it was pretty
clear that even in that story women were treated as possessions. Either side of

safety was treated as proof of a man's worth[^1].
